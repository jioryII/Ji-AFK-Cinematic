A major immersion update introducing dedicated fishing and sleep cinematics, improved camera composition and expanded Minecraft version support.

## Added

- Added **Fishing Cinematics**, a new cinematic mode designed specifically for fishing.
  - Cinematics can begin automatically after casting a fishing rod, with a configurable activation delay of **10 seconds by default**.
  - Regular cinematic shots continue while waiting for a catch.
  - When a fish approaches the hook, the camera automatically switches to a close fishing-focused composition.
  - Mouse movement, clicks, retrieving the rod and casting again no longer interrupt the fishing cinematic.
  - Walking or pressing **Esc** still exits the cinematic normally.

- Added **Sleep Cinematics**, allowing cinematic presentation and black bars to remain active while sleeping.
  - **Light Mode** keeps the sleeping interface and chat available.
  - **Full Mode** hides the sleeping interface for a cleaner cinematic presentation.
  - Designed to complement extended and immersive sleeping mods such as **Better Days** and **VO: True Sleep**.

## Improved

- Improved the balance between **player-focused and environment-focused shots**.
- Configured camera percentages now produce a more accurate and consistent shot distribution.
- Character and environment shots now frame their intended subjects more reliably.
- Pressing **Esc** during a cinematic now closes the cinematic first instead of immediately opening the pause menu.
- Improved fishing transitions when catching a fish and casting the rod again.

## Fixed

- Fixed cinematic black bars not displaying correctly while sleeping.
- Fixed fishing cinematics being cancelled by mouse movement, left click, right click or retrieving the rod.
- Fixed missing fish-approach camera framing on some Minecraft 1.21 versions.
- Fixed crashes when activating fishing cinematics on Minecraft **1.21.11**.
- Fixed world-loading crashes affecting Minecraft **26.1, 26.1.1 and 26.1.2**.
- Improved compatibility across Minecraft **1.21–1.21.11** and **26.1–26.3**.
