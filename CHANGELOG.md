# Ji-AFK Cinematic v2.3.0

An evolved cinematic experience with stronger direction, smoother presentation and
greater control.

## Highlights

- **Cinematic redesign:** 30 carefully composed character and environment shots.
- **Persistent modes:** choose between Normal, Interactive and Persistent `[BETA]`.
- **Consistent camera motion:** linear movement that remains stable at any shot duration.
- **Personalized composition:** adjustable balance between character and environment shots.
- **Refined atmosphere:** subtle camera rotation and more balanced music selection.
- **Reliable across versions:** improved stability throughout Minecraft 1.21–1.21.11 and 26.1–26.2.
